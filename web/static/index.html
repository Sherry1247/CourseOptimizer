    How to use

    From a blank page to an advisor-ready plan in four steps

    1. 1

      Set your timeline and majors

      Choose first-year or transfer, your first term and graduation term, up to three majors, and any AP, IB or transfer credit. A transcript can be imported and reviewed before anything is added.

      Open plan settings →
    2. 2

      Build the plan

      BadgerPlan fills each term with required courses, their prerequisites and Gen Ed, schedules shared courses first, and keeps each term under your credit limit.

      Build my plan →
    3. 3

      Drag courses and fix conflicts

      Move courses between terms. Every move is re-checked for prerequisites, usual offering season and credit load, and conflicts are listed with a way to fix them.

      Find courses to add →
    4. 4

      Check requirements and share

      Review a DARS-style requirements check, compare a second major, then save, export or print the plan to bring to your advisor.

      See requirements →

    What BadgerPlan is not

    • It is not DARS. Your official degree audit and your advisor decide what counts.
    • Offerings are historical patterns, not a promise that a course runs in a given term.
    • Grade distributions describe past sections; they do not predict your grade.
    • Rate My Professors scores are a small, self-selected sample of student opinions.
